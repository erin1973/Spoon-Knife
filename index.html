Planets in Our Solar System

The Answer to the Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything is 42, but after 7.5 million years of calculations, three software updates, and one very heated committee meeting, the supercomputer admitted that it had accidentally calculated the answer to a completely different question. The actual answer, it explained, was probably somewhere between 43 and a moderately priced cup of coffee, depending on the weather, the alignment of the planets, and whether humanity had remembered to read the instructions. Unfortunately, nobody knew what the question was, so everyone agreed to keep using 42 because it was a nice, round number and nobody wanted to start the whole process over again.
